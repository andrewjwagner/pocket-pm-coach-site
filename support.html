EMAIL SUPPORT

Common fixes

Sign-in trouble

  • Confirm you are using the same method you originally chose: email/password, Google, or Apple.
  • For email sign-in, check the address for typos and use the password-reset option if needed.
  • If you used Sign in with Apple and hid your email, continue using Apple rather than entering the relay address manually.
  • Check your connection, restart the app, and try again. If it still fails, send us the exact error message.

AI grading or network errors

  • Make sure your device has a stable internet connection; grading requires network access.
  • Wait a moment and retry if the request times out or the service is temporarily unavailable.
  • Keep the app open while feedback is generated and avoid submitting the same answer repeatedly.
  • If an answer repeatedly fails, email support with the drill type and approximate time. Do not include sensitive information.

Drill limits

  • Pocket PM Coach may apply temporary drill or grading limits to manage capacity and keep the service reliable.
  • If you reach a limit, follow the message shown in the app and try again after the stated reset period.
  • If the limit appears incorrect or does not reset, contact support with a screenshot of the message.

Notification settings

  • On iPhone, open Settings → Notifications → Pocket PM Coach and turn on Allow Notifications.
  • Review Focus modes and Scheduled Summary if reminders arrive silently or late.
  • Use the notification or reminder controls available inside Pocket PM Coach to change your preference.

Delete your account

In Pocket PM Coach, open Settings → Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps. This requests deletion of your account and associated app data. If you cannot sign in, email hello@pocketpmcoach.com from the email address linked to your account. See the Privacy Policy for retention details.
